About

I'm a doctoral graduate in Educational Leadership with a Master's in Adult Education and Community Development. My work sits at the intersection of program design, organizational learning, and equity — helping institutions build practices that hold up outside the classroom.

Before and during my graduate studies, I worked directly with adult learners and community organizations, which shaped how I think about leadership: not as authority handed down, but as the capacity to design conditions where people and programs can grow.

I'm currently exploring roles where I can bring research, facilitation, and program design together — in higher education, nonprofit leadership, or applied policy work.
